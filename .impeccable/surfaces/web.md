# Surface brief: SciBox design system and app shell

Mode: Operate (shell, search results, forms). The landing page in slice 12 extends this world.

Scope: tokens, components, header with role switcher, footer, mobile menu, /styleguide.
Audience: scientists and hiring organizations, phone and desktop equally. Job: find and compare positions, read key facts fast, act before a deadline.
Proof/content: fictional demo vacancies with real Russian cities. No invented statistics.
Constraints: Russian, WCAG 2.2 AA, no dark theme for now (daytime office and phone scene), no kicker/eyebrow above headings, no colored side borders, no gradient text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A vacancy is set like an article in a serious science journal, warm and readable, and the one thing to act on (the deadline) is marked with a yellow highlighter. Refuses the generic job-board card grid with logo tiles and a blue button.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white page, warm near-black ink, deep ink-blue (#1C2C66) for actions and links, marker yellow (#F7D559) as the only accent, hairline rules between entries. Literata for titles and abstracts, Golos Text for interface and facts. Square-ish 8-10px corners, no shadows except focus, lists with hairlines instead of boxed cards.
STORY: A scientist sees within seconds what the position is, what level and field, and by when to apply; an organization sees the same pages as its own shop window. Trust comes from calm typography and honest facts.
FIRST VIEWPORT: White header with ink-blue serif wordmark, nav, role switcher as two text tabs with a marker underline on the active one; large serif page title; one wide search field with region select and ink-blue Find button; type filter pills; result count with a black rule; then entries with serif title, org line, two-line abstract, facts row and the deadline marked in yellow on the right.
FORM: Journal / научный журнал, own top-ranked candidate (model pick), seed key 71afb147.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
